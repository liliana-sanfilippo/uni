\subsection*{Step 1. Calculate the leaf distance vectors}
The distance vector for the matrix: 
\[
d^M = 
\begin{pmatrix}
d(A,B) \\ d(A,C) \\ d(A, D) \\ d(A,E) \\d(A,F) \\ d(B,C) \\ d(B,D) \\ d(B,E) \\ d(B,F) \\ d(C,D) \\ d(C,E) \\d(C,F) \\ d(D,E) \\ d(D,F) \\ d(E,F)
\end{pmatrix} = 
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\ 8 \\18\\18\\6\\6\\12\\8\\8\\18\\18\\12\\10\\8\\12
\end{pmatrix} 
\]
And from the given tree: 
\[
d^T = 
\begin{pmatrix}
d(A,B) \\ d(A,C) \\ d(A, D) \\ d(A,E) \\d(A,F) \\ d(B,C) \\ d(B,D) \\ d(B,E) \\ d(B,F) \\ d(C,D) \\ d(C,E) \\d(C,F) \\ d(D,E) \\ d(D,F) \\ d(E,F)
\end{pmatrix} = 
\begin{pmatrix}
4\\6\\8\\8\\6\\6\\8\\8 \\6\\8\\8\\6\\8\\8\\8\\
\end{pmatrix} 
\]

Example on how to calculate $d(A,B)$ easily:

\subsection*{Step 2. Calculate the error (easy)}
Formula: 
\[
E := || d^T - d^M ||^2 = \sum_i
(d^T_i - d^M_i )^2
\]
Here that is
\[
E = (4-4)^2 + (6-8)^2 + (8-18)^2 + ... = 0+4+100+100+0+0+16+0+4+100+100+36+4+0+16 = 480
\]

(A smaller E is better)
